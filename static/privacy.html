Privacy Policy

PulseTrade · Last updated 13 October 2026

PulseTrade is a student portfolio project that shows stock market data alongside news-derived sentiment. This policy explains what it collects and why.

What we collect

  • Account details. If you sign up with email, we store your email address and date of birth. Your password is handled by our authentication provider and is never visible to us. If you sign in with Google, we receive your email address and display name — nothing else from your Google account.
  • Your watchlist. The ticker symbols you save.
  • A session cookie. Used only to keep you signed in between visits.

We do not collect payment details, and we do not use advertising or analytics trackers.

Why we collect it

Your email identifies your account. Your date of birth confirms you meet the minimum age requirement. Your watchlist exists so your saved tickers are there when you return. That is the full extent of it.

Who else sees it

  • Supabase — stores accounts and watchlists.
  • Render — runs the application.
  • Google — if you choose Google sign-in, and for the language model that writes the market outlook text.
  • Alpha Vantage and Yahoo Finance — supply market and news data.

Only the ticker you are analysing is sent to these data and language services. Your email, date of birth, and watchlist are never sent to them. We do not sell your data or share it with anyone else.

Keeping and deleting it

Your data stays until you ask us to remove it. Email the address below to have your account and everything attached to it deleted, or to get a copy of what we hold.

Children

PulseTrade is not intended for anyone under 13, and accounts cannot be created below that age.

Security, honestly stated

Accounts are protected by our authentication provider, and each user can only read their own watchlist. That said, this is a student project, not a regulated financial service. Please do not store anything sensitive here.

Not financial advice

Everything PulseTrade displays is for information only. It is not a recommendation to buy or sell any security.

Changes

If this policy changes, the date at the top changes with it.
